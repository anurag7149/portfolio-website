Portfolio / Project 02
UI/UX CASE STUDY · BABYWEAR E-COMMERCE

FAURA

A soft, emotionally engaging shopping experience designed around modern parents, thoughtful gifting and the little moments that matter.

Role
UI/UX & Visual Design
Scope
Research to responsive UI
Platform
Shopify e-commerce
Duration
4 weeks
01The brief

Designing trust into every interaction.

Buying for a baby is practical and emotional at the same time. FAURA needed an experience that made discovery feel calm, product information feel dependable and the path to purchase feel effortless across desktop and mobile.

01Clarity

Make browsing and product decisions simple.

02Warmth

Reflect the tenderness of the brand without visual clutter.

03Confidence

Use transparent information to support parent decisions.

02Understand

Start with the brand.
Listen to the parent.

The visual system was built around softness, warmth and trust. Research then translated those values into tangible user needs: quality reassurance, useful product detail, mobile-first access and a less overwhelming shopping experience.

“The experience should feel as thoughtful as the products themselves.”

Design principle
03Structure

Turning insight into
a calmer journey.

Research shaped a clear architecture and a low-friction flow—from discovery and evaluation through checkout and delivery. Each decision was tested against a simple question: does this help a parent feel informed and in control?

04Validate

Designed, tested
and refined.

Usability testing focused on the tasks that matter most: finding the right product, understanding details, adding to cart and completing checkout. Feedback was used to clarify hierarchy, strengthen product information and remove friction.

92%Task success rate
88%User satisfaction
76%Faster completion
4.8/5Experience rating
05Reflect

A system built to grow
with the brand.

The result is a cohesive foundation that connects FAURA’s emotional brand language with practical e-commerce needs. The project also defined a clear direction for personalisation, mobile refinement, broader product discovery and future expansion.

SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION

Built with a complete design trail.

Research notes, personas, wireframes, usability plans and final reflections kept the work connected from the first insight to the final interface.
